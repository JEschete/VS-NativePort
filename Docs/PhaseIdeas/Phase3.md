# Vagrant Story: Phase 3 Mods

Phase 3 is the optional, expansive modding phase: new playable characters, weapons, systems, places, stories, and interfaces. Ideas should respect Vagrant Story's established world and underlying combat logic unless a proposal explicitly describes an optional overhaul. Preserve the original experience as a selectable mode when a mod changes core gameplay.

## Weapons, equipment, and appearance

1. **Non-disassemblable relic weapons.** Add named relic weapon families with unique silhouettes, lore, and upgrade paths. Relics are special finished items that cannot be disassembled, keeping them distinct from ordinary workshop components.
2. **Fist weapons.** Add punch daggers, cestuses, and clawed gauntlets as a short-range weapon family, with dedicated textures and models.
3. **Dual-wield small weapons.** Let Ashley equip two small weapons, selected from daggers, shortswords, fist weapons, maces, and axes. Each pairing has its own reach, damage, speed, and defensive trade-offs.
4. **Weapon stance prototypes.** Prototype alternate stances for selected existing weapon types, with each stance changing a few concrete attack properties. Evaluate how stance choice fits the original targeting, chain, and Risk systems before expanding it.
5. **Expanded gem capacity.** Give select rare weapons additional gem capacity beyond the existing weapon-and-shield attachment system.
6. **Unique legendary arms.** Add a small set of discoverable weapons with distinctive appearance, lore, and carefully balanced special abilities.
7. **Cursed equipment.** Add powerful equipment with persistent drawbacks that reward deliberate build choices.
8. **Equipment set bonuses.** Add modest bonuses for combinations of related armor pieces without making individual pieces useless.
9. **Shield techniques.** Add shield-specific reactions, such as a timed bash or projectile deflection, designed alongside rather than in place of existing Defense Abilities.
10. **Additional equipment slots.** Add back and ring slots, with compatible equipment, appearances, and effects.
11. **Equipment weight and agility builds.** Expand the existing agility trade-offs from armor into a more deliberate light-to-heavy equipment progression; treat this as an extension of existing stat behavior, not a brand-new mechanic.
12. **Weapon inscriptions through gems.** Expand gems with unique effects that modify weapons, building on the existing gem system rather than adding a separate inscription system.
13. **Appearance customization.** Make the appearance of equipment customizable beyond weapon naming, including eligible armor and accessory visuals.
14. **Equipment dyeing.** Once armor appearance customization exists, allow eligible pieces to be recolored using pigments found throughout Leá Monde.
15. **Equipment preview.** Add a rotating preview of Ashley wearing equipped armor and holding weapons, if practical within the port's model and UI systems.
16. **Workshop crafting overhaul.** Replace the need to combine many near-identical weapons to reach a desired weapon/class combination with a clearer workshop crafting path using gathered materials and deliberate recipes.
17. **Crafting materials.** Add new metals, monster materials, and occult reagents as drops and exploration finds used by the workshop overhaul.
18. **Advanced workshop recipes.** Add discoverable plans for new weapon families, armor, equipment modifications, and gem effects.
19. **Material refinement.** Let players process gathered materials into higher-grade crafting components through workshop services.
20. **Workshop armor modifications.** Add installable armor modifications with distinct tactical effects, integrated with the existing assembly and repair systems.
21. **Field workshop kit.** Add a limited-use kit that performs a restricted selection of workshop actions outside workshops.
22. **Consumable pouch.** Give usable items a separate, capacity-limited inventory space instead of making them compete with crafting materials.
23. **Expanded inventory slots.** Increase capacity for selected existing item categories and new equipment, with clear per-category limits.

## Character growth, combat, and magic

24. **Stat allocation.** Add a level-up or milestone system that lets players allocate points among new character attributes.
25. **New character attributes.** Define attributes with effects distinct from Strength, Intelligence, Agility, and the existing affinity systems; establish how they interact with limb condition, Risk, magic, and equipment before balancing them.
26. **Talent system.** Add a set of learnable talents with meaningful choices across combat, magic, crafting, and survival, replacing generic Risk-threshold bonuses.
27. **Weapon mastery through affinity.** Extend the existing class and affinity growth with optional milestones or rewards rather than adding a separate repetitive weapon-proficiency track.
28. **Additional Chain Abilities.** Add optional abilities that fit the existing timed chain inputs and create new choices around damage, defense, healing, and Risk.
29. **Additional Break Arts.** Add selected new Break Arts for existing or new weapon types, respecting their established HP cost and weapon-specific progression.
30. **New spell school.** Add a distinct school of magic, such as blood rites or stormcraft, with new grimoires, spells, visuals, and tactical costs.
31. **Spell interactions.** Add a small number of deliberate interactions between existing or new spells, creating setup-and-payoff choices without making the system mandatory.
32. **Weapon-mass pushback.** Let heavy attacks push enemies according to weapon mass and target size, with restrained displacement that suits the compact rooms and existing encounter layouts.
33. **Environmental enemy effects.** Add enemies and encounters that make use of existing environmental hazards or alter the local fight in bounded ways.

## Enemies and bosses

34. **Possessed effigies.** Add small, fast enemies that resemble religious statuary and attack when Ashley approaches.
35. **Aquatic enemies.** Populate a flooded area with creatures whose range, movement, and affinities make the environment matter without requiring a new water-physics system.
36. **The Hollow Penitents.** Add paired enemies in which one pressures Ashley while the other prepares support or a counterattack; introduce a healer variant and reserve full group encounters for a companion-enabled mode.
37. **Compact-room enemy tactics.** Add bounded flanking, supporting, and retreat behaviors to selected enemies, designed for the game's mostly small encounter spaces.
38. **Elite variants.** Add uncommon enemies with adjusted affinities, attacks, or targetable-part vulnerabilities, and increase their item drop rates.
39. **Custom undead boss variants.** Expand the existing precedent of enemies changing form, such as the Minotaur becoming the Minotaur Zombie, with new zombie-boss designs and custom textures.
40. **Boss phase gates.** Use HP thresholds to trigger new boss behaviors, attacks, or target priorities while keeping each phase readable and rooted in the existing battle system.
41. **Rosencrantz encounters.** Make greater use of Rosencrantz through additional story scenes or a carefully staged recurring encounter that reflects his role and abilities.
42. **Hazard-focused boss.** Use a new boss encounter to explore existing or modded floor hazards such as poison and explosion tiles, giving Ashley ways to recognize and respond to the danger.
43. **Expanded bestiary.** Enrich enemy entries with discovered lore, habitats, affinities, targetable parts, and behavior beyond the current concise status information.

## New places, stories, and playable characters

44. **The Drowned Ward.** Explore a partially submerged neighborhood beneath Leá Monde, with flooded chambers and waterlogged homes.
45. **The Bell Foundry.** Add an abandoned industrial complex where mechanisms and heavy structures shape traversal and encounters.
46. **The Royal Archive.** Build a forbidden library of sealed records, hidden rooms, and discoveries about Valendia and Leá Monde.
47. **The Ashen Gardens.** Create an overgrown cemetery garden with ash-covered paths, roots, and ruined statuary.
48. **The Lower Aqueduct.** Add a branching underground waterway with sluice-gate puzzles.
49. **The Mirror Cloister.** Introduce reflective chambers with visual illusions and routes that complicate navigation.
50. **The Pilgrim's Quarter.** Add ruined inns, chapels, and shrines that reveal how travelers once entered the city.
51. **The Founders' Crypt.** Open an ancient burial complex beneath Leá Monde's oldest foundations.
52. **The Smuggler's Warrens.** Add concealed passages and abandoned hideouts tied to the city's history.
53. **The Star Chamber.** Create a late-game observatory with celestial instruments and a multi-room puzzle.
54. **Riskbreaker case files.** Add optional investigations that send Ashley through new and existing rooms to uncover evidence and secrets.
55. **Merlose chapter.** Make Callo Merlose playable in a self-contained chapter with abilities and challenges suited to her perspective and capabilities.
56. **Sydney chapter.** Make Sydney Losstarot playable in an optional story that explores his abilities and perspective without replacing Ashley's story.
57. **Additional playable characters.** Create further playable characters with distinct abilities, motivations, and storylines.
58. **Companion-enabled group encounters.** Introduce companions in a dedicated mode or chapter, enabling enemy group fights designed around allies rather than altering Ashley's solo campaign.
59. **Alternate story route.** Add an optional route through new rooms that changes encounters and reveals additional context while leaving the main route intact.
60. **Environmental storytelling.** Add inscriptions, abandoned workspaces, and visual traces that deepen the history of Leá Monde.

## Gems, professions, and progression systems

61. **Gem fusion.** Allow duplicate gems to be combined into stronger variants through the workshop.
62. **Gem-crafting profession.** Add a profession skill that improves gem combination and determines how much affinity is lost during fusion.
63. **Gem resonance.** Give selected combinations of equipped gems additional effects, with resonance interacting with gem fusion and its crafting skill.
64. **Unique gem effects.** Expand gems to grant new, distinctive effects such as weapon coatings or other special properties.
65. **Workshop alchemy.** Let players combine reagents into new single-use tonics with effects distinct from existing consumables.
66. **New status effects.** Add effects such as bleed, corrosion, and frostbite. Define bleed distinctly—for example, direct damage on physical actions or attacks—while poison remains damage over time, and balance both against existing status effects.
67. **Talent specializations.** Let players deepen selected talent paths in weapon use, spellcraft, crafting, or survival, with meaningful trade-offs.
68. **New Game+ rule variants.** Build optional challenge rules on top of the existing New Game+ system, preserving the established replay mode and its access to later content.
69. **Difficulty presets.** Add balanced difficulty choices that alter enemy behavior and resource pressure, not merely enemy health.
70. **Ironman mode.** Add an opt-in run with limited saves and no reloading after defeat.
71. **Loadout presets.** Save several equipment and ability configurations for quick selection outside active combat; this can move to Phase 2 if treated as a general usability improvement rather than a content mod.

## Interface, customization, and modding

72. **Full interface replacement.** Offer a modern, resizable UI skin that replaces the original menus while retaining their functions.
73. **Battle HUD layouts.** Provide configurable arrangements for combat meters, target information, and ability prompts.
74. **Equipment comparison overlay.** Compare selected items side by side, including existing affinity and class effectiveness and any new attributes.
75. **Expanded quest journal.** Track optional investigations, discovered clues, and player-selected objectives without spoiling undiscovered content.
76. **Expanded lore archive.** Build on the game's existing story and information records with browsable character, place, and document entries.
77. **Collectible training-dummy workshop.** Add a custom practice workshop where each training dummy becomes available after the player finds and destroys its original counterpart.
78. **Accessibility settings.** Add optional chain-timing assistance, clearer targeting, subtitle controls, and remappable inputs.
79. **Photo mode.** Allow camera adjustment and scene capture in safe exploration areas, with optional UI removal.
80. **Expanded subtitle options.** Add text size, background, speaker labels, and timing controls for dialogue and cutscenes.
81. **Custom audio support.** Document and expose the port's existing support for custom sounds rather than adding a separate dynamic music system.
82. **Community mod framework.** Document safe data formats and loading for player-created weapons, enemies, areas, UI skins, and challenge rules.

## Optional combat evolution overhaul

83. **Real-time combat mode.** Develop an optional combat overhaul alongside the original mode. Remove the target sphere and the pause in which Ashley and enemies choose actions, but keep the original combat available.
84. **Real-time dodging and blocking.** Add responsive dodge and block actions for the overhaul, with clear commitment and recovery so defense remains a tactical decision.
85. **Revised enemy AI.** Rework enemy behavior for continuous combat, including attacks that can be read and answered without the original targeting pause.
86. **Combat skills for real-time play.** Adapt or replace selected player abilities so they make sense in continuous movement and defense, without simply porting pause-based actions unchanged.
87. **Stamina or action-resource model.** Test a resource that constrains repeated dodging, blocking, or attacks, if needed to balance the new combat flow.
88. **Preserve strategic depth.** Keep meaningful decisions around weapon choice, class and elemental affinities, enemy anatomy, Risk, positioning, and resource use in the real-time mode.
89. **Combat-mode balance pass.** Test responsiveness, enemy readability, difficulty, and fairness across weapon types and encounters; make this overhaul independently selectable from the original combat.
